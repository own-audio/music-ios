# Favorites and ratings

## Favorites

You can make songs, albums and artists favorites. They are kept on the server, so other apps
signed in to the same account see them too.

- A song: the star next to the title in the player, or **Favorite** in the song's menu.
- An album or an artist: the star at the top right of its page.

A favorite song has a small star in song lists. **Library → Favorites** lists them all, split
into **Songs**, **Albums** and **Artists**, with **Play** and **Shuffle** for the songs. The
albums list can also be sorted by **Favorites**.

## Ratings

On servers with ratings (own.audio and Subsonic servers such as Navidrome; not Jellyfin), songs
and albums can have one to five stars. Choose **Rate** in a song's or album's menu, then the
number of stars. The menu shows the rating you gave (**Rating: ★★★★**), and **Clear Rating**
takes it away.

Ratings are used by **Top Rated** in the album list, and on own.audio by smart playlists such as
**Back in Rotation** and **Rating at Least** in a rule.

A favorite or a rating made while offline is kept and sent when the server can be reached
again.
