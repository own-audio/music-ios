# Lock screen, widgets, Siri, AirPlay and CarPlay
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Lock screen and Control Center

Music keeps playing with the app in the background or the phone locked. The lock screen,
Control Center and headphone controls show the song and cover, and have play, pause, next,
previous and the position bar to move in the song. After a phone call or Siri, the music comes
back where it was.

## AirPlay

The AirPlay button at the end of the player's tools row picks where the sound plays: the
iPhone, Bluetooth headphones or an AirPlay speaker. You can also pick it in Control Center.

## Widget

The **Recently Played** widget shows where you left off and the albums and playlists you played
last: one on the small widget, three on the medium one. Tap one to open the app and play it. Add
it the usual way: touch and hold the home screen, then the add button, then own.music.

## Siri and Shortcuts

The app works with Siri and the Shortcuts app without any setup. You can say:

- "Continue listening in own.music" or "Resume my music in own.music": goes on with what is in
  the player, or the queue from before.
- "Play my playlist Road Trip in own.music" or "Play Road Trip in own.music".
- "Play the album Kind of Blue in own.music" or "Listen to Kind of Blue in own.music".

Albums and playlists are looked up on the selected server. In Shortcuts, the actions are
**Continue Listening**, **Play a Playlist** and **Play an Album**.

## Background updates

The app asks iOS to let it update now and then in the background, about once an hour, so the
library is current when you open it: it brings the copy of the library on the phone up to date
and sends plays and changes that were waiting. iOS decides when, and whether, it runs.

## CarPlay

The app has CarPlay support, with **Recently Played**, **Recently Added**, **Downloads**,
**Playlists**, **Artists** and **Favorites**, and the system's Now Playing screen. It has not
been tried in a car yet, so treat it as untested. If no server is added, it says **Add a server
on your iPhone**.
