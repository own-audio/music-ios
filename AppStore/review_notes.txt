No account is needed to try the app: on the welcome screen, "Try the Demo Library" signs in to the public own.audio demo server (demopi.own.audio) by itself. It holds sample music; open Library → Albums and play one.

The app plays music from servers the listener runs or rents: own.audio, and any Subsonic or Jellyfin server. A reviewer would need their own Subsonic or Jellyfin server for those; everything in the app works the same way on each.

Some parts appear only when the server offers them: the family and its shared songs, listening statistics, identifying songs with MusicBrainz, and sign-in with Apple or Google. The app sells nothing and links to no purchase.

Deleting an account: Settings → the server → Delete Account… The demo's shared account is protected by the server, so deleting it is refused with an explanation; on any other own.audio account it deletes the account and everything it added.

The app is free and open source (MPL-2.0): https://github.com/own-audio/music-ios
