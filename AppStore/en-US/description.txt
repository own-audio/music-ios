A native player for the music on your own server: Navidrome and other Subsonic or OpenSubsonic servers (Gonic, Airsonic and more), Jellyfin, and own.audio.

Plays the way music should
• Gapless playback, so live and classical albums flow as recorded, or crossfade if you prefer
• Lossless FLAC and ALAC, with the format shown in the player
• Starts playing while the song is still arriving
• Volume leveling from your files' ReplayGain values, or Jellyfin's measured loudness
• Six-band equalizer with presets
• Synced lyrics that follow the song, where your server has them
• Sleep timer

Your whole library
• Artists, albums, songs, genres, playlists and search
• Recently added, recently played, most played, favorites, highest rated
• Favorite songs, albums and artists; rate songs and albums where the server keeps ratings
• Create and edit playlists
• Plays are reported to your server

Offline
• Download albums, playlists or single songs
• Keep the whole library on the iPhone, to browse it with no signal
• On mobile data: the original, a smaller stream, or downloads only

At home and away
• Several servers at once
• A server's home and outside addresses: the app uses whichever answers, and only if it is the same server
• AirPlay, lock screen and Control Center
• iPhone and iPad, in English and Czech

With an own.audio server
• Smart playlists, chosen anew each time from what you have listened to; shared with the household, everyone gets their own selection
• Identify your library with MusicBrainz, review what it proposes, undo anything
• The library on the iPhone catches up within seconds of a change

Private by design: the app talks only to the servers you add, and to Apple or Google if you sign in with them. No account with us, no analytics, no ads. A Subsonic password stays in the iPhone's Keychain and only a one-time token made from it is sent; Jellyfin and own.audio keep just a sign-in token.

Free and open source under the MPL-2.0. Made by own.audio.
