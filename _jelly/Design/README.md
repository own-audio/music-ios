# Icon

`AppIcon.svg` is the source of the app icon: the own.audio app-icon construction (the five
family dots around the app's symbol) in Jellyfin's purple-to-blue, with `</>` in the ring's
open gap at the bottom to say the app is open source. It is on the light ground the other
own.audio apps use; `AppIcon-dark.svg` is the dark-mode variant. Render each at 1024 × 1024
with no rounded corners and no alpha channel (App Store Connect refuses one); iOS rounds
them. The welcome screen shows the same artwork (`AppLogo` in the asset catalog, 360 px).

A placeholder: it is too close to own.audio for Subsonic's orange icon, and both are to be
redesigned.
