A native music player for your Jellyfin server.

Plays the way music should
• Gapless playback, so live and classical albums flow as recorded, or crossfade if you prefer
• Lossless FLAC and ALAC, with the format shown in the player
• Starts playing while the song is still arriving
• Volume leveling from the loudness your server measured
• Equalizer with presets
• Synced lyrics that follow the song, where your server has them

Your whole library
• Artists, albums, songs, genres, playlists and search
• Recently added, recently played, most played, favorites
• Instant Mix: songs like this song, album or artist, picked by your server
• Create and edit playlists; mark favorites
• Plays are reported to your server, as with every Jellyfin app

Offline and on the go
• Download albums, playlists or single songs and play them with no signal
• Smaller streams on mobile data if you want them; Wi-Fi stays original

Everywhere
• Sign in with your password or Quick Connect
• Several servers at once, one music library or all of them
• AirPlay, lock screen and Control Center
• iPhone and iPad, in English and Czech

Private by design: the app talks only to the servers you add. Your password is used once to sign in and isn't stored. No account, no analytics, no ads.

Free and open source under the MPL-2.0. Made by own.audio. Not affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
