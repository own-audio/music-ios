# Icon

`AppIcon.svg` is the source of the app icon: the own.audio construction — the five family
figures around the app's symbol, each in its own colour as in the own.audio mark
(`#FABB05`, `#29ABE2`, `#FF3B30`, `#FFCC00`, `#34C759`) — with the music note in red
(`#FF3B30`; `#FF453A` on the dark ground, as iOS brightens its system red there). It is on the
light ground the other own.audio apps use; `AppIcon-dark.svg` is the dark-mode variant. Render each at
1024 × 1024 with no rounded corners; iOS rounds them. The welcome screen shows the same
artwork (`AppLogo` in the asset catalog, 360 px).
